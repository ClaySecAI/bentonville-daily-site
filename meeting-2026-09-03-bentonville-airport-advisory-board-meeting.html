Bentonville Airport Advisory Board Meeting

When
Thursday, September 3, 2026, 3:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW "A" Street, Bentonville

The Airport Advisory Board will receive updates on several ongoing airport construction projects, including taxiway extensions and apron expansions.

Item IV.A

Board to receive updates on airport engineering and construction projects

Residents will learn about the progress of several projects, including the East Taxiway Extension, Hangar Access Road, East Apron Expansion, West Taxiway Rehabilitation, West Turf Apron, Perimeter Fence Installation – Phase 1, and West Taxiway Extension, some of which are funded by the FAA, ADA, or BIL.

Item IV.B

Board to hear a status report from Legends Air Center

This item provides an update on the operations and activities at Legends Air Center, which is a key part of the airport's services.

Item IV.C

Board to receive updates from the City of Bentonville on airport operations and events

This includes information on airport webpage upgrades, upcoming events like the Game Aerospace & Bell Helicopter Event, the Arkansas Airport Operators Association Conference, fuel tank cleaning, the Snow and Ice Management Plan, Eastside Hangar Development, crane operations near I-49 S, and the 2026 Budget.

Item VI.A

Board to confirm the next meeting schedule

This item sets the date, time, and location for the next Airport Advisory Board meeting, which will be Thursday, October 1, 2026, at 3:00 pm in City Hall – Council Chambers.
