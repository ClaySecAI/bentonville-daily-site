Bentonville Airport Advisory Board Meeting

When
Thursday, September 3, 2026, 3:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW "A" Street, Bentonville

This meeting will review updates on several airport construction projects and general airport operations.

Item IV.A

Airport Advisory Board to receive updates on several airport construction projects

Residents would learn about the progress of the East Taxiway Extension (Phase 2), a Hangar Access Road, East Apron Expansion, West Taxiway Rehabilitation, West Turf Apron, Perimeter Fence Installation – Phase 1, and West Taxiway Extension. Some of these projects are funded by the FAA, some by ADA with 80/20 or 90/10 splits, and one by BIL.

Item IV.B

Airport Advisory Board to receive a status report from Legends Air Center

Residents would hear an update on the operations and activities of Legends Air Center at the airport.

Item IV.C

Airport Advisory Board to receive updates on various airport operations and events

Residents would learn about Airport Webpage Upgrades, the Game Aerospace & Bell Helicopter Event from September 9-13, the Arkansas Airport Operators Association Conference from September 21-23, Fuel Tank Cleaning, Snow and Ice Management Plan Feedback, Eastside Hangar Development Update, and cranes along I-49 S in conjunction with Alice Walton Hospital Campus (Ref # 2026-ASW-10595-OE), as well as a 2026 Budget Update.

Item VI.A

Airport Advisory Board to confirm the next meeting schedule

Residents would be informed that the next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, October 1, 2026, at 3:00 pm, at City Hall – Council Chambers – 305 SW “A” Street, Bentonville, AR.
