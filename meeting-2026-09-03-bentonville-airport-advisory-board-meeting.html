Bentonville Airport Advisory Board Meeting

When
Thursday, September 3, 2026, 3:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW "A" Street, Bentonville

This meeting will review several ongoing airport construction projects and receive updates on airport operations and future plans.

Item IV.A

Airport Advisory Board to receive updates on several airport engineering and construction projects

Residents would learn about the progress of projects like the East Taxiway Extension, Hangar Access Road, East Apron Expansion, West Taxiway Rehabilitation, West Turf Apron, Perimeter Fence Installation – Phase 1, and West Taxiway Extension, some of which are funded by the FAA, ADA, or BIL.

Item IV.B

Airport Advisory Board to receive a status report from Legends Air Center

This item would provide an update on the operations and activities of Legends Air Center at the airport.

Item IV.C

Airport Advisory Board to receive updates from the City of Bentonville Airport Manager

Residents would hear about various airport activities and plans, including Airport Webpage Upgrades, upcoming events like the Game Aerospace & Bell Helicopter Event and the Arkansas Airport Operators Association Conference, Fuel Tank Cleaning, the Snow and Ice Management Plan, Eastside Hangar Development, and a 2026 Budget Update, as well as information on cranes along I-49 S related to the Alice Walton Hospital Campus.

Item VI.A

Airport Advisory Board to schedule its next meeting

This item sets the date, time, and location for the next Airport Advisory Board meeting, which will be Thursday, October 1, 2026, at 3:00 pm in the City Hall – Council Chambers – 305 SW “A” Street, Bentonville, AR.
