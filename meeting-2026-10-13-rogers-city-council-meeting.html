Rogers City Council Meeting

When
Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 6:30 p.m.

The City Council will consider approving grant agreements to fund design and environmental phases for road improvements and pedestrian infrastructure.

Item 13

Council to approve a grant for design and environmental phases of improvements to the 1st Street and Pleasant Grove Road intersection

This would allow the city to receive a $133,600.00 grant and appropriate an additional $33,400.00 from street fund reserves to begin planning for improvements at the intersection of 1st Street and Pleasant Grove Road.

Amount
$133,600.00, $33,400.00

Item 14

Council to approve a grant for design, environmental, and right-of-way phases of Highway 71B pedestrian improvements

This would allow the city to receive a $422,188.00 grant and appropriate an additional $105,547.00 from street fund reserves to fund the initial phases of pedestrian improvements along Highway 71B.

Amount
$422,188.00, $105,547.00
