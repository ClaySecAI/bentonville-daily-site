Rogers City Council Meeting

When
Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 6:30 p.m.

The City Council will consider several resolutions to accept grant funding and amend the 2026 budget for various projects, including transportation improvements and a tree giveaway.

Item 13

Council to accept $133,600 grant for 1st Street and Pleasant Grove Road intersection improvements

This would fund the design and environmental phases for improvements at the intersection of 1st Street and Pleasant Grove Road, with $133,600 coming from a grant and $33,400 from Street Fund Reserves.

Amount
$133,600.00, $33,400.00

Item 14

Council to accept $422,188 grant for Highway 71b pedestrian improvements

This would fund the design, environmental, and right-of-way phases for pedestrian improvements along Highway 71b, with $422,188 from a grant and $105,547 from Street Fund Reserves.

Amount
$422,188.00, $105,547.00

Council to accept $11,407.50 grant for Fall Tree Giveaway

Amount
