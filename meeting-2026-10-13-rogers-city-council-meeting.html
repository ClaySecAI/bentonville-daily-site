Rogers City Council Meeting

When
Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 6:30 p.m.

The Rogers City Council took up several resolutions to authorize grant agreements and amend the 2026 budget for various projects, including transportation improvements and a tree giveaway.

Item 13

Council took up a resolution authorizing a transportation grant agreement for 1st Street and Pleasant Grove Road improvements.

This resolution proposed authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into a grant agreement with the Northwest Arkansas Regional Planning Commission and the Arkansas Department of Transportation to receive $133,600.00 for the design and environmental phases of improvements to the intersection of 1st Street and Pleasant Grove Road. It also proposed amending the 2026 budget to recognize these grant funds and appropriate $33,400.00 from Street Fund Reserves for the project.

Amount
$133,600.00, $33,400.00

Item 14

Council took up a resolution authorizing a transportation grant agreement for Highway 71b pedestrian improvements.

This resolution proposed authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into a grant agreement with the Northwest Arkansas Regional Planning Commission and the Arkansas Department of Transportation to receive $422,188.00 for the design, environmental, and right-of-way phases of the Highway 71b Pedestrian Improvements. It also proposed amending the 2026 budget to recognize these grant funds and appropriate $105,547.00 from Street Fund Reserves for the project.

Amount
$422,188.00, $105,547.00

Item 6

Council took up a resolution authorizing a grant agreement for the City’s Fall Tree Giveaway.

This resolution proposed authorizing the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into a grant agreement with the Kelly And Steuart Walton Fund to receive $11,407.50 for the City’s Fall Tree Giveaway. It also proposed amending the 2026 budget to recognize these grant funds and appropriate the same amount for the Foresting The Future Tree Giveaway.

Amount
$11,407.50

Item 7

Council took up a resolution to amend the 2026 budget for miscellaneous revenue and fire equipment.

This resolution proposed amending the 2026 budget to recognize miscellaneous revenue in the amount of $10,703.00 from the sale of assets and appropriate that same amount for Fire Hose And Foam.

Amount
$10,703.00

Item 8

Council took up a resolution to amend the 2026 budget for fleet expenses.

This resolution proposed amending the 2026 budget to appropriate $16,554.00 from General Fund Reserves into the Fleet account.

Amount
$16,554.00

Item 9

Council took up a resolution to amend the 2026 budget for insurance reimbursements and fleet expenses.

This resolution proposed amending the 2026 budget to recognize insurance reimbursements in the amount of $20,576.96 and appropriate that same amount into the Fleet account.

Amount
$20,576.96
