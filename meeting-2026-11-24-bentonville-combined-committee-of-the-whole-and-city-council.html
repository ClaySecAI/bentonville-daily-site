Bentonville Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council Meeting

When
Tuesday, November 24, 2026, 6:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW A St, Bentonville

The Bentonville Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council took up several rezoning ordinances and an ordinance accepting the Trailblazers Annexation.

Item IV.1

Council took up an ordinance to rezone property at 608 NW B Street.

The proposed ordinance would change the zoning classification of the property from R-1, Suburban Single-Family to T3.1, Neighborhood Edge. The Planning Commission voted 7-0 to recommend approval of this rezoning (Case RZ26-0050).
