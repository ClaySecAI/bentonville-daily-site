Bentonville Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council Meeting

When
Tuesday, August 25, 2026, 6:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW A St, Bentonville

The Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council took up several significant items, including multiple resolutions to accept grant funding for road improvements and an ordinance to waive competitive bidding for the purchase of 161 kV breakers.

Item III.1

Council took up an ordinance approving a waiver of bid for the purchase of 161 kV breakers.

This ordinance proposed waiving competitive bidding to purchase four 161 kV breakers from GE Grid Solutions for $690,400.00 plus tax, due to volatility in the electric industry. The Utility Board approved this 3-0.

Council took up an ordinance to rezone property at 301 SE B Street.

This ordinance proposed changing the zoning classification of 301 SE B Street from R-1, Suburban Single-Family, to T3.2, Neighborhood Transition. The Planning Commission voted 7-0 to recommend approval of this rezoning (Case RZ26-0039).
