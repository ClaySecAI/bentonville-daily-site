Item I.2

Board to waive competitive bids for 750 copper underground wire for the Tiger Overpass

This ordinance would allow the Utility Board to purchase 750 copper underground wire from Stuart Irby without competitive bidding, due to market volatility and lead times for these products.

Board to authorize a change order to add various equipment line items to IFB 24-29

This resolution would modify an existing bid award to include additional equipment line items, allowing contractors to install or rebuild full overhead lines for larger jobs beyond what the original hourly rates cover.
