Bella Vista City Council Work Session

When
Monday, August 17, 2026, 6:00 p.m.
Where
Bella Vista District Court, Bella Vista

The City Council will consider reducing the prepared food and beverage tax rate from one percent to one-half of one percent.

Item IV.C

Council to reduce the prepared food and beverage tax rate

This proposed ordinance would decrease the tax you pay on prepared food and beverages from one percent to one-half of one percent. This means you would pay less tax when purchasing meals and drinks from local establishments.

Ordinance
Case

Council to appropriate funds for public safety building additions

This resolution would amend the 2026 City Budget to allocate $155,000.00 from development impact fee revenue. These funds are intended for additions to public safety buildings.

Item III.B

Council to consider requiring orientation and continuing education for elected officials

This ordinance, on its second reading, would establish mandatory orientation and ongoing training for elected officials in Bella Vista. It would also set minimum training requirements and outline how compliance will be reported.

Council to appropriate funds for library growth-related expenditures

This resolution would amend the 2026 City Budget to appropriate $31,653.00 from development impact fee revenue. These funds are designated for qualifying expenditures related to the growth of the library.

Type
Resolution
Amount

Item IV.D

Council to establish an advisory Green Committee

This proposed ordinance would create a new advisory Green Committee for the city. It would define who can be a member, how the committee will be organized, and what its duties will be.
