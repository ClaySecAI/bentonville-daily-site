Bentonville School District reports 6% vaccine exemption rate

Data from the Arkansas Department of Education shows that 1,204 students in the Bentonville School District are exempt from vaccinations this school year, representing about 6% of total district enrollment. Officials state that exemption rates topping 5% in some Northwest Arkansas school districts threaten herd immunity.
