Bentonville Finance Committee Meeting

When
Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 5:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW 'A' Street, Bentonville

The Bentonville Finance Committee took up resolutions concerning grants for the "I" Street Water Supply Transmission Main Extension, the Bentonville Public Library, and the Eagle Way Tunnel & Trail.

Item 3

The committee took up a resolution accepting a grant for the "I" Street Water Supply Transmission Main Extension.

This resolution proposed accepting a grant from the Walton Family Foundation for up to $6,683,590.00 to support construction of the "I" Street Water Supply Transmission Main Extension. The Utility Board had approved this 5-0, and a budget adjustment was needed.
