The Bentonville Utility Board took up an ordinance to waive competitive bidding for the purchase of 161 kV breakers and a resolution for a recycling partnership grant agreement.

Item I.1

The Board took up an ordinance approving a waiver of bid for the purchase of 161 kV breakers.

This ordinance concerned waiving competitive bidding to purchase four 161 kV breakers from GE Grid Solutions for $690,400.00 plus tax, citing volatility in the electric industry and market conditions. A budget adjustment was needed for this purchase.

Item I.2

The Board took up a resolution authorizing a recycling partnership grant agreement.

This resolution concerned entering into a grant agreement with The Recycling Partnership, Inc. for grant funds not to exceed $202,000.00, along with access to in-kind services up to $125,000.00, to increase participation and capture of recyclable materials from the city’s residential curbside collection program.
