Bentonville Library Advisory Board Meeting

When
Wednesday, November 4, 2026, 5:00 p.m.
Where
Bentonville Public Library, Bentonville

The Bentonville Library Advisory Board took up several routine library reports and discussed upcoming vacancies.

Item 3

Board took up the 'America 250: What I Love About America' artwork project.

The board discussed an initiative inviting Bentonville students in 7th through 12th grade to submit original two-dimensional artwork illustrating what they love about America for the country’s 250th anniversary.

Item 1

Board took up pending vacancies on the Library Advisory Board.

The board discussed upcoming vacancies effective December 31, 2026, including Kimberly Seay, Nathan Pollett, and Michelle Evans, who is eligible for reappointment.

Item 2

Board took up various library reports, including programming, marketing, administration updates, and public art approval.

The board received updates on programming and marketing from Tara Cloud Clark, administration updates and BPL statistics regarding 20 years at 405 South Main Street from Hadi Dudley, and discussed public art approval for the 'America 250 – What I Love About America' project.

Location
405 South Main Street

Item 1

Board took up details regarding the 'America 250' student artwork installation.

The board discussed the proposed installation of 6” x 6” individual student artworks from grades 7-12, combined into one mural, which was approved by the Public Art Advisory Committee on Oct. 15 and was pending City Council approval on Nov. 10 for installation by the end of the year.

Item 3

Board took up standing reports from ex-officio groups.

The board received reports from the Friends of the Bentonville Library and the BPL Teen Advisory Board (TAB), including an update from Jemal Janyyeva.
