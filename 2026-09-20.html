On the Bella Vista City Council agenda: Council to reduce the prepared food and beverage tax rate

This proposed ordinance would amend Ordinance No. 2017-15 to decrease the tax on prepared food and beverages from one percent (1%) to one-half of one percent (0.5%). This change would mean residents pay less tax on prepared food and beverage purchases.
