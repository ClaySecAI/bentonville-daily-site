The Bentonville Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council took up several ordinances, including the annexation of surrounded territory and amendments to the municipal code.

This ordinance proposed annexing unincorporated territory completely surrounded by the city limits, making it part of Bentonville and assigning it to a ward.
