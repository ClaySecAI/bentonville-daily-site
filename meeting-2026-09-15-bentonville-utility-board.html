Bentonville Utility Board

When
Tuesday, September 15, 2026, 11:30 a.m.
Where
305 SW 'A' Street, Bentonville

The Bentonville Utility Board took up several resolutions and an ordinance related to financing, agreements for services, and project bids.

Item I.2

The board took up a resolution of intent to finance a portion of the 48" Supply Water Transmission Project.

This resolution concerned financing a portion of the project with bonds purchased by the Arkansas Development Finance Authority in the amount of $9,621,360.00, with $887,911.00 of the principal amount in the form of a principal forgiveness bond.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$9,621,360.00, $887,911.00

Item I.5

The board took up a resolution to enter into an agreement with RJN Group for SSES & Modeling Work within the SLS Basin.

This resolution authorized the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into an agreement with RJN Group, Inc. in the amount of $865,530.00 for SSES and modeling work within the South LS Basin.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$865,530.00

Item I.1

The board took up an ordinance amending the repayment schedule and disbursement cut-off date for the 2025 Bond.

This ordinance proposed amending the repayment schedule and Disbursement Cut-Off Date for the Combined Electric, Water and Sewer System Revenue Bond, Series 2025, to allow the City to fully expend the Series 2024 Bond bearing interest at 1.75% annually before spending the Series 2025 Bond bearing interest at 4% annually.

Type
Ordinance

Item I.3

The board took up a resolution authorizing easement acquisition associated with the Shewmaker Ph. 2 Interceptor Project.

This resolution authorized the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into agreements to purchase easements associated with the Shewmaker Ph. 2 Interceptor Project.

Type
Resolution
