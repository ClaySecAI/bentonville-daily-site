Bentonville Utility Board

When
Tuesday, September 15, 2026, 11:30 a.m.
Where
305 SW 'A' Street, Bentonville

The Bentonville Utility Board will consider financing for a major water transmission project and several significant infrastructure improvements.

Item I.2

Board to finance a portion of the 48" Supply Water Transmission Project

This resolution would allow the city to finance a portion of a large water transmission project with bonds purchased by the Arkansas Development Finance Authority, including a principal forgiveness bond of $887,911.00.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$9,621,360.00, $887,911.00

Item I.5

Board to approve an agreement with RJN Group for SSES and modeling work in the South LS Basin

This agreement would authorize the city to contract with RJN Group, Inc. for $865,530.00 to identify and quantify sources of inflow and infiltration, provide conceptual rehabilitation design, and expand the city's existing hydraulic model in the South LS Basin.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$865,530.00

Item I.4

Board to award a bid for the Townbranch Interceptor Project - Alignment 1

This resolution would award a bid of $7,605,314.85 to Crossland Heavy Contractors for the construction of Alignment 1 of the Townbranch Interceptor project.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$7,605,314.85

Item I.1

Board to amend the repayment schedule and disbursement cut-off date for the 2025 Bond

This ordinance would change the repayment schedule and disbursement cut-off date for the Combined Electric, Water and Sewer System Revenue Bond, Series 2025, to allow the city to fully expend the Series 2024 Bond (1.75% interest) before spending the Series 2025 Bond (4% interest).

Type
Ordinance

Item I.3

Board to authorize easement acquisition for the Shewmaker Ph. 2 Interceptor Project

This resolution would allow the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into agreements to purchase easements needed for the Shewmaker Ph. 2 Interceptor Project.

Type
Resolution
