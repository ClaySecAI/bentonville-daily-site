Bentonville Planning Commission Meeting

When
Tuesday, November 17, 2026, 4:30 p.m.
Where
Community Development Building, Bentonville

The Planning Commission will consider several rezoning requests that could change the types of housing allowed in specific areas of the city.

Item IV.1

Commission to consider rezoning 608 NW B Street from single-family to neighborhood edge

This proposed change would allow for a different type of development than suburban single-family homes at 608 NW B Street, moving towards a more mixed-use neighborhood style.

Case
RZ26-0050
Location
608 NW B Street

Item IV.2

Commission to consider rezoning 306 NW C Street from single-family to neighborhood transition

This rezoning request for 306 NW C Street would shift the property from suburban single-family housing to a type that allows for a transition into an urban neighborhood.

Case
RZ26-0051
Location
306 NW C Street

Item IV.3

Commission to consider rezoning property at SW Parnell Dr. at SW Naphtali Blvd from planned residential to walkable neighborhood

This proposed rezoning would change the development potential of the property at SW Parnell Dr. at SW Naphtali Blvd from a planned residential development to a type that supports a more walkable neighborhood environment.

Case
RZ26-0052
