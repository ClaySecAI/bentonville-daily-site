On the Bella Vista City Council agenda: Council to consider reducing the prepared food and beverage tax rate

This proposed ordinance would amend Ordinance No. 2017-15 to reduce the tax on prepared food and beverages from one percent (1%) to one-half of one percent (0.5%). This change would mean residents pay less tax on restaurant meals and other prepared food items.

On the Bentonville City Council agenda: Council to rezone 201 SW E Street from R-1, Suburban Single-Family to T3.1, Neighborhood Edge

This proposed change would allow for a different type of development at 201 SW E Street than the current suburban single-family zoning permits, potentially leading to more varied housing options in the neighborhood.

On the Bentonville City Council agenda: Council to accept $4,000,000.00 in grant funds for Greenhouse Road Improvements

Accepting these grant funds would help pay for construction of improvements on Greenhouse Road between Hwy 12 and Hwy 102, with the city contributing 55% of a $1,000,000.00 local match.
