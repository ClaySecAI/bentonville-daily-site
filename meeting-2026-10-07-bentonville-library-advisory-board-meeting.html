Bentonville Library Advisory Board Meeting

When
Wednesday, October 7, 2026, 5:00 p.m.
Where
Bentonville Public Library, Bentonville

The Library Advisory Board will discuss updates to library policies, including those related to Arkansas Act 372, and address upcoming vacancies on the board.

Item E

Library to prioritize resources compatible with existing hardware and software

This item outlines the criteria the library would use when acquiring new resources, considering compatibility with current systems, licensing, and network access, ideally allowing access beyond the physical library building.

Item 1

Library to receive updates on programming, marketing, and administration, including policy changes and board vacancies

Residents would learn about upcoming library programs and marketing efforts, as well as proposed updates to library policies related to Arkansas Act 372 and the two (2) upcoming vacancies on the Library Advisory Board by December 31, 2026.

Item 2

Reports from the Friends of the Bentonville Library and the BPL Teen Advisory Board

The board would hear updates from groups that support the library and represent the interests of teen patrons, including a report from Jemal Janyyeva of the BPL Teen Advisory Board.

Item 3

Reports from various community organizations

The board would receive updates from the Bentonville Library Foundation, the Bentonville History Museum, the Northwest Arkansas Genealogical Society, Northwest Arkansas Community College, and Bentonville Schools, which could highlight collaborative efforts or community needs related to the library.

Library reaffirms commitment to 'Freedom to Read' and 'Library Bill of Rights'

This item indicates the library's adherence to principles that ensure a wide range of materials are available to the public and protect intellectual freedom.
