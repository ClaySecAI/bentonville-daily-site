On the Bentonville Utility Board agenda: Board to waive competitive bidding for 161 kV breakers

This would allow the Bentonville Utility Board to purchase four 161 kV breakers from GE Grid Solutions for $690,400.00 plus tax, without seeking other bids, for future substation projects. This decision is proposed due to market volatility and long lead times for these products.
