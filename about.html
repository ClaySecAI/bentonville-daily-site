AboutBentonville Daily

What Bentonville Daily is

Bentonville Daily is a daily brief of public records and local institutions in Bentonville, Bella Vista, Rogers and Benton County. It reads the documents that civic life actually runs on and points you to them. It summarises in its own words and links out to the source; it does not republish other outlets' reporting.

How it is made

The brief is assembled by a program, not typed up by a person each morning. Every day it reads its sources, drafts an item for each thing worth knowing, and writes the brief. Before anything is published a second, separate pass checks each claim against the source it came from. Anything that pass flags is held back for a human to look at rather than published. A day can end with nothing new, and that is fine.

Facts that a reader might act on, like a dollar figure or a case number on a meeting agenda, are copied straight from the document rather than written by the model, and shown apart from the summary so you can tell which is which.

What it covers, and what it does not

It covers institutions and public records: city council and commission agendas, ordinances and filings, weather alerts, and local civic news. It does not cover crime, charges, arrests or court cases, and it never prints a person's name in connection with one. Bentonville Daily will name an applicant on a filing, an official acting in office, or a business in its own announcement, because that is ordinary local news. The line it holds is between naming someone and accusing them.

Sources

Meeting agendas and minutes come from the cities' own public records systems. Weather alerts come from the National Weather Service. Local news items come from area outlets' public feeds, and each links back to the outlet that reported it.
