On the Bentonville City Council agenda: Council to rezone 501 N Main Street from suburban single-family to neighborhood edge

This proposed rezoning would change the classification of the property at 501 N Main Street from R-1, Suburban Single-Family to T3.1, Neighborhood Edge, which could allow for a different type of development than single-family homes.

Source: Bentonville City Council agenda

City Hall

On the Rogers City Council agenda: Council to accept a grant for a workforce housing infrastructure pilot program

This resolution would authorize the city to accept grant funds up to $5,000,000.00 from the Walton Family Foundation for a pilot program aimed at workforce housing infrastructure. The 2026 budget would be amended to recognize the first installment of $3,000,000.00.

Source: Rogers City Council agenda
