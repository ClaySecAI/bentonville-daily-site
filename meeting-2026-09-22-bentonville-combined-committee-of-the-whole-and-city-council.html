Bentonville Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council Meeting

When

The Bentonville Combined Committee of the Whole and City Council took up several resolutions, including one to accept $4,000,000.00 in grant funds for Greenhouse Road improvements.

Council took up an ordinance to rezone property at 201 SW E Street.

This ordinance proposed changing the zoning classification of the property from R-1, Suburban Single-Family to T3.1, Neighborhood Edge. The Planning Commission had previously voted 7-0 to recommend approval.
