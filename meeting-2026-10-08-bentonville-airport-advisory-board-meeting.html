Bentonville Airport Advisory Board Meeting

When
Thursday, October 8, 2026, 3:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW "A" Street, Bentonville

The Airport Advisory Board will receive updates on several airport construction projects, including taxiway extensions, apron improvements, and a new perimeter fence.

Item IV.A

Board to receive updates on airport engineering and construction projects

Residents would be interested in the progress of projects like the East Taxiway Extension, Hangar Access Road, East Parking Apron, West Taxiway Rehabilitation, West Turf Apron, Perimeter Fence Installation – Phase 1, and West Taxiway Extension, which impact airport operations and infrastructure.

Item IV.B

Board to receive a status report from Legends Air Center

This update would inform the board about the current operations and any developments at Legends Air Center, a key airport tenant.

Item IV.C

Board to receive updates on city airport matters

This item includes updates on the Arkansas Airport Operators Association Conference, Eastside Hangar Development, Airport Board Candidate Selection, and the Aviation Engineering Consulting Contract, which could affect the airport's future development and management.

Item VI.A

Board to note the cancellation of the November meeting and schedule the next meeting

This item informs residents about the upcoming meeting schedule for the Airport Advisory Board, with the November meeting cancelled and the next meeting set for Thursday, December 3, 2026, at 3 PM in City Hall.
