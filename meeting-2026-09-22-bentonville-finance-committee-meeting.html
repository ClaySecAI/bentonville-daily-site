Bentonville Finance Committee Meeting

When
Tuesday, September 22, 2026, 5:00 p.m.
Where
305 SW 'A' Street, Bentonville

The Finance Committee will consider accepting several grants for road, trail, and intersection improvements, as well as a grant to cover pet adoption fees.

Item 4

Committee to accept a $185,605 grant for the John DeShields Trail design and bidding

This would fund the design and bidding documents for trail improvements along the John DeShields Trail corridor between NE J Street and E Central Avenue.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$185,605.00

Item 5

Committee to accept a $1,026,589 grant for the SW "I" Street and SW 41st Street intersection

This would fund improvements at the intersection of SW "I" Street and SW 41st Street, including the installation of a traffic signal.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$1,026,589.00

Item 1

Committee to accept a $6,000 grant to sponsor pet adoptions

This would cover the $75 dog adoption fee and the $50 cat adoption fee for an estimated 80 pet adoptions for the remainder of 2026.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$6,000.00, $75.00, $50.00

Item 3

Committee to recognize a budget adjustment for West McNelly Road Bridge repair

This would formally account for the funds already spent on necessary repairs to the West McNelly Road Bridge.

Type
Resolution
