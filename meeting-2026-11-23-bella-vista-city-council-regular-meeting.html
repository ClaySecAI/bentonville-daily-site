Bella Vista City Council Regular Meeting

When
Monday, November 23, 2026, 6:00 p.m.

The City Council is asked to approve new regulations for short-term rentals in Bella Vista.

Item IX.D

Council to consider new rules for short-term rentals

This ordinance would amend the city's zoning code to add specific regulations regarding the density and use of short-term rentals, which could affect property owners who operate or plan to operate them.

Type
Ordinance

Item IX.H

Council to approve contract for Riordan Road/US 71 intersection improvements

The City Council is asked to approve a contract for $69,395.00 with Garver, LLC for professional engineering services related to improvements at the Riordan Road/US 71 intersection.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$69,395.00

Item VIII.C

Council to consider requiring approval for license plate readers and facial recognition systems

This ordinance, on its third reading, would prohibit the City of Bella Vista from acquiring or activating automatic license plate reader systems or facial recognition and identification systems without prior approval from the City Council.

Type
Ordinance

Item IX.E

Council to reallocate $14,200.00 in unspent capital funds for fire truck and ambulance repairs

This resolution would amend the 2026 city budget to move $14,200.00 from unspent capital funds for miscellaneous equipment to cover repairs for fire trucks and ambulances.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$14,200.00, $5,000.00

Item IX.F

Council to move 2026 budget work session date

This resolution would amend the 2026 City Council meeting schedule to move the budget work session from Tuesday, December 1st to Wednesday, December 2nd.

Type
Resolution

Item IX.G

Council to set meeting schedule for 2027

This resolution would establish the official meeting schedule for the Bella Vista City Council for the calendar year 2027.

Type
Resolution
