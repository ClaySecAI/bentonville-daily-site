Bella Vista City Council Regular Meeting

When
Monday, November 23, 2026, 6:00 p.m.

The City Council is asked to approve new rules for short-term rentals and to require Council approval before the city can acquire or activate license plate readers or facial recognition systems.

Item IX.D

Council to amend zoning ordinance for short-term rentals

This ordinance would change the city's zoning rules to include specific regulations for the density and use of short-term rentals.

Type
Ordinance

Item IX.H

Council to approve contract for Riordan Road/US 71 intersection improvements

This resolution would authorize a contract with Garver, LLC for $69,395.00 to provide professional engineering services for improvements at the Riordan Road/US 71 intersection.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$69,395.00

Item VIII.C

Council to require prior approval for license plate reader or facial recognition systems

This ordinance would prohibit the city from acquiring or activating an automatic license plate reader system or a facial recognition and identification system without first getting approval from the City Council.

Type
Ordinance

Item IX.E

Council to amend 2026 city budget for fire truck and ambulance repairs

This resolution would amend the 2026 city budget by $14,200.00, reallocating unspent capital funds from miscellaneous equipment to cover fire truck and ambulance repairs.

Type
Resolution
Amount
$14,200.00, $5,000.00

Item IX.F

Council to reschedule 2026 budget work session

This resolution would change the 2026 City Council meeting schedule, moving the budget work session from Tuesday, December 1st to Wednesday, December 2nd.

Type
Resolution

Item IX.G

Council to set 2027 meeting schedule

This resolution would establish the official meeting schedule for the Bella Vista City Council for the calendar year 2027.

Type
Resolution
