The Rogers City Council took up a resolution to accept a grant of up to $5,000,000.00 for a workforce housing infrastructure pilot program.

Item 3

Council took up a resolution to accept a grant for a workforce housing infrastructure pilot program.

The resolution authorized the Mayor and City Clerk to enter into a grant agreement with the Walton Family Foundation to accept grant funds up to $5,000,000.00 for a Workforce Housing Infrastructure Participation Pilot Program. It also proposed amending the 2026 budget to recognize the first installment of grant funds in the amount of $3,000,000.00.

$5,000,000.00, $3,000,000.00
