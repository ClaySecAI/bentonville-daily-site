Bentonville Public Library to receive a quarterly payment

The Bentonville Public Library qualifies for a first-quarter payment of $21,983 for Fiscal Year 2027. This payment helps fund library operations and services.

Library Advisory Board to consider accepting a quilt donation

The Library Advisory Board is asked to approve accepting a gift from the NWA Modern Quilt Guild and allow the quilt to be publicly displayed at the Bentonville Public Library. This decision would also require approval from the Public Art Advisory Council and the City Council.

The Library Advisory Board will hear standing reports from the Friends of the Bentonville Library and the BPL Teen Advisory Board. These reports provide updates on activities and initiatives supporting the library.
