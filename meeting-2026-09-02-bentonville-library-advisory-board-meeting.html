Bentonville Public Library qualifies for a first quarter payment

The Bentonville Public Library is eligible to receive a payment of $21,983 for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027.

Library Advisory Board to consider accepting and displaying a quilt donation

The board is asked to approve the acceptance of a gift from the NWA Modern Quilt Guild and allow it to be publicly displayed at the Bentonville Public Library. This approval is also subject to review by the Public Art Advisory Council and the City Council.
