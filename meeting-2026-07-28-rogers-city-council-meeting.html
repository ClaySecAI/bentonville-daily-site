Rogers City Council Meeting

Tuesday, July 28, 2026

The council took up several resolutions involving budget amendments for street and transportation work, a tree canopy grant agreement, and a utility easement.

Item 1

Council took up reports of boards and standing committees

The council heard reports from its boards and standing committees.

Item 1

Council took up a budget amendment reallocating transportation street fund appropriations

The resolution proposed to amend the 2026 budget by reallocating certain appropriations within the transportation street fund.

Item 2

Council took up a transportation contract with Building Exteriors, LLC and a budget amendment

The council considered authorizing the mayor and city clerk to enter into a contract with Building Exteriors, LLC of Springdale, Arkansas, and amending the 2026 budget to appropriate $113,620.00 from the Street Fee In Lieu of Reserves.

Item 3

Council took up a budget amendment for transportation street fund expenses

The resolution proposed to amend the 2026 budget to appropriate $17,000.00 from the Transportation Street Fund Fee In Lieu of Reserves.

Item 4

Council took up a right-of-way and utility easement agreement with Carroll Electric Cooperative Corp.

The council considered authorizing the mayor and city clerk to enter into a right-of-way and utility easement agreement with Carroll Electric Cooperative Corp. of Berryville, Arkansas.

Item 5

Council took up a matching grant agreement with the Walton Family Foundation and a budget amendment

The council considered authorizing the mayor and city clerk to enter into a 50/50 matching grant agreement with the Walton Family Foundation to receive up to $815,087.00, and amending the 2026 budget to recognize and appropriate $200,000.00 into the General Fund-Parks account for a tree canopy grant.

Amount
$815,087.00, $200,000.00
